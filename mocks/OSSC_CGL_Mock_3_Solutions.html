1. Arithmetic - 10th Standard
Q.1 Find the value of √1.5625.
ANSWER: OPTION B (1.25)
Method: (1.25)² = 1.25 * 1.25 = 1.5625. For decimals, √15625 = 125, so for 4 decimal places inside, we have 2 places outside.
Q.2 Smallest number to divide 3600 to make it a perfect cube?
ANSWER: OPTION B (450)
Expansion: Prime factors of 3600 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 × 5.
To be a cube, we need 2³. Extra factors are 2 × 3² × 5² = 2 × 9 × 25 = 450.
2. Data Interpretation (Charts, Graphs, Tables, Data Sufficiency etc.) - 10th Standard
Q.26 Highest growth rate crop (2022-23)?
ANSWER: OPTION A (Oilseeds)
Growth = (Final - Initial)/Initial * 100.
Oilseeds: (18-15)/15 = 20%. Pulses: (22-20)/20 = 10%. Rice: 6.25%.
3. Logical Reasoning and Analytical Ability, General Mental Ability
Q.46 Man walks 5km North, Right 3km, Right 5km. Distance?
ANSWER: OPTION B (3km)
The path describes three sides of a rectangle. He starts at (0,0), goes to (0,5), then (3,5), then (3,0). Distance from start (0,0) is 3km.
