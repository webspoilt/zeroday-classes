1. Arithmetic - 10th Standard

Q.1-25
Q.1
A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become 4 times itself at the same rate?
A 16 years
B 20 years
C 24 years
D 32 years
Logic: In 8 years, interest earned = P. To become 4P, total interest needed = 4P - P = 3P. Since SI is proportional to time, Time = 8 years × 3 = 24 years.
Q.2
A shopkeeper allows a 20% discount on the marked price of an item and still makes a profit of 20%. If the cost price of the item is ₹1,200, what is its marked price?
A ₹1,600
B ₹1,800
C ₹1,500
D ₹2,000
Step 1: CP = 1200, Profit = 20% → SP = 1200 × 1.2 = 1440. Step 2: Discount = 20% → 80% of MP = 1440. Step 3: MP = 1440 / 0.8 = ₹1,800.
Q.3
A can complete a work in 15 days, while B can do it in 20 days. They work together for 6 days and then A leaves. In how many more days can B finish the remaining work?
A 6 days
B 8 days
C 4 days
D 10 days
Work rates: A=1/15, B=1/20. Together: (1/15+1/20) = 7/60. In 6 days: 6×(7/60) = 7/10. Remaining work = 1 - 7/10 = 3/10. B takes: (3/10) / (1/20) = 6 days.
Q.4
The price of sugar increases by 32%. A family reduces its consumption so that the expenditure on sugar increases by only 10%. If the initial consumption was 33 kg per month, what is the new monthly consumption?
A 25.5 kg
B 28.2 kg
C 30 kg
D 27.5 kg
Expenditure = Price × Quantity. 1.1E = 1.32P × NewQ. Since E = P × 33, NewQ = (1.1 × 33) / 1.32 = 36.3 / 1.32 = 27.5 kg.
Q.5
A person travels from A to B at 40 km/h and returns from B to A by increasing his speed by 50%. What is his average speed for the entire journey?
A 45 km/h
B 50 km/h
C 48 km/h
D 42 km/h
Return speed = 40 + 20 = 60 km/h. Average speed = 2xy/(x+y) = (2 × 40 × 60) / (40 + 60) = 4800 / 100 = 48 km/h.
Q.6
The speed of a boat in still water is 15 km/h and the speed of the stream is 3 km/h. The distance traveled downstream in 12 minutes is:
A 1.2 km
B 3.6 km
C 2.4 km
D 4.0 km
Downstream speed = 15 + 3 = 18 km/h. Time = 12/60 = 0.2 hours. Distance = 18 × 0.2 = 3.6 km.
Q.7
A train moving at 54 km/h crosses a platform in 30 seconds. If the length of the train is 150m, what is the length of the platform?
A 250m
B 350m
C 300m
D 450m
Speed in m/s = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s. Total distance (Train + Platform) = 15 × 30 = 450m. Platform = 450 - 150 = 300m.
Q.8
A sum of ₹10,000 is invested in two parts. One part at 8% simple interest and the other at 10%. If the total annual interest is ₹920, how much was invested at 8%?
A ₹6,000
B ₹5,000
C ₹4,000
D ₹7,000
Let x be at 8%. 0.08x + 0.10(10000-x) = 920 → 0.08x + 1000 - 0.10x = 920 → -0.02x = -80 → x = 4000.

Q.23
A, B and C can do a piece of work in 20, 30 and 60 days respectively. In how many days can A do the work if he is assisted by B and C on every third day?
