Q.2
What is the smallest number by which 3600 should be divided to make it a perfect cube?
A 45
B 450
C 50
D 300
3600 = 2^4 * 3^2 * 5^2. To get a cube, divide by 2 * 3^2 * 5^2 = 450.
Q.3

A 120m
B 180m
C 150m
D 200m

B 23

D 20
