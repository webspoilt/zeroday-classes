1. Arithmetic (Questions 1 - 25)
Q.1 Value of √1.5625?
ANSWER: OPTION B (1.25)
Logic: Ignore the decimal: √15625 = 125. Since there are 4 decimal places inside the root, there will be 2 decimal places in the result. So, 1.25.
Q.2 Smallest number to divide 3600 to make it a perfect cube?
ANSWER: OPTION B (450)
Prime Factorization: 3600 = 2^4 * 3^2 * 5^2.
To be a cube, powers must be multiples of 3. We keep 2^3.
Leftover = 2^1 * 3^2 * 5^2 = 2 * 9 * 25 = 450.
Q.3 Train length crossing a pole in 9s at 60 km/h?
ANSWER: OPTION C (150m)
Speed in m/s = 60 * 5/18 = 50/3 m/s.
Length = Speed * Time = (50/3) * 9 = 150 meters.
